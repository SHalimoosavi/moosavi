SYJ-MINE

A self-hosted infrastructure platform for controlled and observable RandomX mining operations, with explicit authorization controls, operator telemetry and cross-platform execution.

Why it matters

SYJ-MINE treats mining as an infrastructure and operations problem: authorization, observability, reproducibility and operator control are part of the system boundary.
