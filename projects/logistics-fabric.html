SYJ Logistics Fabric

A federated interoperability layer for logistics systems, designed around independently owned nodes, self-hosting and interoperable supply-chain data exchange.

ENGINEERING BUILD Go · Go 1.22+ · Pure Go · Apache 2.0 · Termux
Repository ↗

Why it matters

The project explores distributed logistics infrastructure without assuming that every participant should surrender control of its operational data to one central platform.
