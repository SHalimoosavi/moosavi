SYJ Nexus Sentinel Audit

A zero-dependency, cross-platform security observability daemon designed to identify devices on a local network, flag unrecognized devices and maintain a local audit trail.

ENGINEERING BUILD TypeScript · Node.js · Drizzle ORM · Linux · Windows · macOS · Termux

Why it matters

Nexus Sentinel Audit focuses on practical local security visibility without requiring a cloud SIEM or heavyweight native agent stack.
